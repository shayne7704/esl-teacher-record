⚠️ Important: The salary displayed above is an estimate. The final salary may still change because the applicable USD/PHP exchange rate can be different when the salary is processed or released.
💡 How the calculation works:

Entered classes use your current tier's USD rate. The USD amount is converted to PHP using the current USD/PHP exchange rate.

GP Slots are fixed at ₱20 each, while Student No-Shows are fixed at ₱26 each. These two amounts are already in Philippine pesos and are therefore not converted using the USD exchange rate.

After adding all earnings, the CO fee is calculated at 30%, leaving 70% as the teacher salary.
